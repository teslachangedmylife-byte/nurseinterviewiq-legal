Privacy Policy

NurseInterview IQ · Updated November 16, 2026

Overview

NurseInterview IQ is designed for private interview practice. The developer is identified on the App Store product page. Updated November 16, 2026. This policy describes the current app build; Apple’s services and external websites have their own privacy policies.

Practice content on your device

Typed or dictated answers, drafts, saved sessions, self-assessment scores, favorites, checklist selections, and preferences are stored locally. The app does not send that practice content to a developer-operated server or a third-party AI provider. Device or cloud backups may include local app data depending on your settings. The app does not create a user account.

Microphone and speech recognition

Microphone and speech access are requested only when you choose a speech feature. The app enables speech-to-text only when Apple reports that on-device English recognition is supported, and it requires on-device processing for that request. If capture is unavailable, you can type. The optional record-and-playback tool keeps a temporary audio recording for rehearsal and removes it when reset or the interview screen is left. Transcribed text can become part of a locally saved answer. Apple controls the system permission prompts and its operating-system services.

On-device AI and spoken questions

Interview follow-ups and coaching use Apple’s on-device model where available. No paid cloud AI endpoint is connected, and interview text is not sent by the app to a cloud model. Prepared follow-ups and reflection prompts are used when the model is unavailable or generation cannot complete. Spoken questions use Apple’s speech-synthesis service and installed voices.

Purchase and preview eligibility

Apple processes the one-time payment and maintains its purchase records. The app receives signed StoreKit transaction information, such as product identifiers, purchase status, and revocation information, to unlock premium tools and restore purchases. It does not receive your full card number. The app stores preview start and last-seen times in the device Keychain to administer the one-time 24-hour home preview. This record is not used for advertising or tracking and is separate from your practice history.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

This build includes no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, cross-app tracking, or developer-operated content-collection endpoint. Apple may provide its own diagnostics, App Store analytics, or purchase services according to your device/account settings and Apple’s policies. We do not sell practice content or use it for targeted advertising.

Retention, export, and deletion

Practice data is retained locally until you erase it or remove the app, subject to device-backup behavior. More includes export and erase controls. Erasing practice data does not remove your Apple purchase or reset preview eligibility. Refunds and Apple purchase records are managed through Apple. Keychain eligibility information may remain after reinstalling. Apple purchase records and backups are managed through Apple’s controls.

Permissions and choices

You can decline speech permissions and continue with typed answers. You can revoke permissions in system settings, disable automatic spoken questions, avoid AI by using classic prepared practice, and restore a purchase or request a refund from Apple. Do not enter patient information or other confidential content.

Links, support, and contact

External links open third-party websites, which receive normal browser/network information under their own policies. If you contact support voluntarily, the information you send is handled by the chosen email or support provider to answer your request. Avoid including sensitive interview or patient details. Contact the developer through the App Support link on the App Store product page or TeslaChangedmyLife@gmail.com.

Policy changes

If the app’s collection or processing practices change, this policy and relevant App Store privacy disclosures will be updated. Review the current policy in More or on the App Store product page.
