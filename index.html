Support and legal information for NurseInterview IQ, a nursing interview preparation app.

One payment. No subscription.

Downloading the app and viewing the home page are free, with no time limit. Skip the welcome purchase screen to view home. Tapping a premium action opens the one-time purchase screen. There is no free trial or automatic charge. The App Store purchase sheet shows the current localized price before you confirm.

Need help?

Email TeslaChangedmyLife@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue. Do not include patient information, passwords, payment details, or confidential interview content.

Restore a purchase

Open More → Restore purchases using the Apple Account that made the purchase. A refunded or revoked purchase may no longer unlock access. For Apple billing or refund help, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Speech and local AI

The app requires iOS 17 or later. On-device AI requires iOS 26 or later, a supported Apple Intelligence device, and a ready model. Prepared practice is available when the model is unavailable. English speech-to-text requires supported on-device recognition and microphone/speech permissions; typing remains available.

Your practice data

Saved answers and drafts are stored on your device. More includes export and erase controls. Erasing practice data does not remove your Apple purchase . Review the Privacy Policy for details.
